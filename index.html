Release notes

Version 2.0 Current

13 November 2026 · Business Request BRQ-RT-7643827 (Auto Loan Calculator Enhancement)

New

  • Vehicle category: choose Brand New or Pre-owned. The form changes to show only the fields that apply (ALC-02).
  • Electric/Hybrid question: answering Yes applies the lower EHV rates and opens the 12 to 60 month terms (ALC-02, Annex 1).
  • Downpayment field and slider: they stay in step with each other. They unlock once a valid vehicle price is entered. Minimum is 20% for brand new and 30% for pre-owned (ALC-02, ALC-03.1).
  • Desired Monthly Payment: enter a monthly budget to get the vehicle price and loan amount you can afford over 60 months (48 months for pre-owned) (ALC-03.1).
  • Instant estimates: the downpayment and monthly payment update as you type, before you click Compute (ALC-03.1).
  • Add-on loan related fees: chattel mortgage fees (upfront or financed) and comprehensive motor insurance by body type (first year or multi-year, upfront or financed) (ALC-03.1).
  • Results by loan term: one table covers every term available to you, with sideways scrolling on small screens (ALC-04).
  • Messages that follow your choices: the note under the table and the "pay before vehicle release" box match the ten fee scenarios in Annex 2 (ALC-04).
  • Dealer quote comparison: enter a dealer's monthly quote to see how much you save (ALC-04 4.1).
  • Accordion stepper: the form is split into three steps that open one after another. Authors can switch the stepper off (ALC-05).
  • Comma formatting: thousands separators appear as you type or paste, and the cursor stays in place (ALC-03.2).
  • Pre-filled from other tools: link to the page with ?category=new&ehv=yes&price=1500000 to fill in the form from Auto Finder or Pre-Qualification (ALC-02 #3).
  • Quick tools carousel and tooltips on each field.

Changed

  • Compute and Reset stay disabled until the required fields are filled in (ALC-02 #4).
  • The results area shows only its headers until you click Compute (ALC-02 #5).
  • The interest rates come from the regular and EHV tables in Annex 1 instead of the V1 promo rates.

Assumptions (to confirm with the business)

  • The insurance rates by body type are placeholders, because Annex 4 was not attached to the request.
  • Chattel mortgage fees are 1.85% of the loan amount, the same rate V1 uses for its loan related fees.
  • Each later insurance year is priced on a vehicle value 10% lower than the year before.
  • The maximum downpayment is 80%. The minimum Desired Monthly Payment is PHP 8,000.
  • Instead of locking the Vehicle Price field when the downpayment is below the minimum (ALC-03.1 2.3c), V2 shows an error.
